Section notes (v2)

Main-site patterns adopted. Pure-black base, sections that fade from black into Tech Blue → Wave Blue, 8px cards with a thin Wave Blue border and a blue glow at the bottom, centred 600-weight H2s, pill buttons (white on dark, blue in the nav), round icon buttons, blue/grey pagination bars.
Global header / footer. Match the homepage: black header with logo, search, menu and a round blue language button; footer fades from black to blue, has column links and a white "Discuss Your Project →" pill, with the copyright on blue. These are still stand-ins for the live global components.
0 · Sub-nav. Black glass bar under the global header. The active anchor is white with a blue underline. Round language and display-mode buttons, blue pill "Request Trial →". On mobile the links become a scrolling row.
1 · Hero. Full-bleed dark hero like the homepage (slot for the main-site background video or photo). 600-weight H1 with "CMS Platform." in blue, white pill CTA (full width on mobile). Cards A/B use the blue-border card style and sit on the corners of the mockup.
2 · Features. Modelled on the homepage "IT Outsourcing" and solution cards: /01 index at top-left, round glass icon at top-right, title and body. 3×2 grid on desktop, stacked on mobile.
4 · How It Works. Modelled on the "Practices" cards: blue gradient cards with a large glass icon peeking from the bottom-right corner, blue step number and connector line. On mobile they become a swipe carousel with pagination bars.
5 · Brain. Layers are an accordion like the homepage FAQ / "Why Newwave" (the open item gets a blue gradient). The open layer lights up in the L1/L2/L3 diagram. Four green-check chips below.
6 · Integrations. Split layout with a divided check list. Hub diagram with black nodes and blue outlines, dashed connectors and travelling pulses. On mobile: the AI node plus a 4×2 grid.
7 · Transparent Data Usage. Blue gradient trust band (like the homepage stats band) with concentric rings and a glass shield icon.
Section rhythm. Dark-led with light breaks, as on the homepage: Hero (dark) → Features (light) → How It Works (dark) → Brain (light, with the diagram kept on a dark panel) → Integrations (dark) → Data Usage (blue) → Footer (dark). Light sections use Slate 50 → Light Blue, white cards with a Slate 200 border, Ink headings, a Wave Blue 600 eyebrow and Light Blue chips.
